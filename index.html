DR. JUAN C. CUEVAS-TELLO

I'm Ph.D. in Computer Science by the University of Birmingham, UK (2004-2007). A postdoctoral researcher at the Instituto Tecnológico y de Estudios Superiores de Monterrey (ITESM), campus Monterrey (2014-2015). Now, I'm working as a professor-researcher at the Universidad Autónoma de San Luis Potosí, Engineering Faculty (Since 2000 up to date), where I'm Research Coordinator at the Research Center and Postgraduate studies (CIEP) at Engineering Faculty, and Coordinator of the Bioinformatics Unit at Research Center in Health Sciences and Biomedicine (CICSaB).
