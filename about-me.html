ABOUT ME

I specialize in artificial intelligence algorithms, including expert systems, artificial neural networks, evolutionary computation, and fuzzy logic—techniques now commonly grouped under machine learning, deep learning, data science, data mining, big data, and business intelligence. My research interests extend to AI applications across diverse fields: bioinformatics, astrophysics, pattern recognition in computer vision, industrial electrical engineering problems and cyber security.
